# Competitive-site research prompt

Use this prompt with a web-research assistant when expanding the Al-Ramk benchmark set:

> Research 8–12 current websites relevant to endurance horse-racing technology and race operations. Prioritize direct products for endurance-race timing, transponders, GPS tracking, veterinary/heart-rate checks, live results, and event management. Include adjacent equestrian timing/results platforms only when clearly labeled as adjacent. Do not use generic horse-riding brands as direct competitors.
>
> For every site, verify the official product/company URL and cite the exact pages used. Record: (1) direct competitor or adjacent reference, (2) audience and primary promise, (3) product capabilities and proof shown, (4) site/page structure, (5) app or live-results experience, (6) lead/download CTA, (7) visual system and use of race imagery, (8) mobile/accessibility observations, and (9) any currentness or evidence limits. Separate statements visible on the site from your own interpretation; do not infer certification, adoption, performance, or market share.
>
> Then synthesize patterns that could improve Al-Ramk’s five-page English static site: home-page hierarchy, feature proof, race-day workflow explanation, app presentation, trust-building without invented testimonials, and contact conversion. Provide 5–8 specific recommendations with cited examples. Do not copy competitor text, code, logos, screenshots, or photography. Cite each factual observation with a direct URL and access date; label unavailable or unverified details instead of guessing.
>
> Suggested search angles: “endurance horse racing timing software live results”; “endurance equestrian GPS transponder timing system”; “endurance ride veterinary gate heart rate software”; and “horse race timing app official results”. Search product vendors and race/event organizers separately so the final sample is not dominated by generic equestrian sites.
